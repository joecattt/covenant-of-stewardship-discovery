# Contact and Accountability Route

**Status: LIVE as of 2026-09-12.**

## How to raise a concern

**Email: whitehat@joecattt.com**

Written concerns are accepted at that address. No physical mailing address is published for
this project; correspondence is by email.

## Who this page is for

- Journalists, researchers, or academics seeking clarification about the corpus or its contents
- **Anyone who believes this framework, its author, or a person acting in its name has caused harm**
- Family members, legal counsel, or advocates concerned about a participant
- Regulatory bodies, legal authorities, or oversight institutions
- Anyone who has encountered impersonation or counterfeit use of the corpus or its marks
- Good-faith critics who prefer to raise a concern without public confrontation

## What to expect

Per the corpus's own commitments:

- Good-faith concerns will be acknowledged and reviewed.
- Concerns involving credible harm to a vulnerable person are treated as high priority.
- Concerns involving impersonation or counterfeit provenance are treated as security matters.
- **No retaliation.** The corpus separates critique of conduct from personal attack, protects
  good-faith criticism raised through proper channels, and classifies retaliation against a
  person for raising a concern as a breach of the covenant itself.
- Personal degradation, harassment, or bad-faith contact will not receive a substantive
  response.

This is a small project run by one person. Expect a human reply, not a ticketing system, and
allow time.

## If you are in crisis

This project is not an emergency service, and its corpus is not medical, legal, or
mental-health advice. In the United States: call or text **988** (Suicide and Crisis
Lifeline), or **911**.

## Substantive criticism gets published

Criticism that lands is added to the published objections alongside the corpus, attributed or
anonymous as you prefer. The critiques already published there were commissioned by the
author and printed unedited, including the parts that land hardest.

## Note on this address and the v1.1 corpus text

Founding Corpus v1.1 describes this address as an internal, emergency-only escalation route
and cautions against advertising it publicly. The author has since designated it as the
public contact channel. The corpus text is frozen as a historical baseline and has not been
edited to match — see `CORPUS-BASELINE.md`. The discrepancy is recorded rather than hidden.
